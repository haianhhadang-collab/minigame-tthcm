Voyage of the Bell

60 questions · 12 ports · 5 question types: reading comprehension, text structure, paragraph summary, multiple choice, fill in the blank.

I am the Bell. The lecture says Spanish friars placed whole towns "bajo de la campana (under the sound of the church bells)", and used me for labor drafts, tributes and religious instruction. I have rung from the friar towns of Cebu to the streets of Manila in 1986, and I have heard many versions of this story.

Today I will carry you through twelve ports, five questions at each, from the camphor traders of Barus to the megachurches of Singapore. After every answer I read you the exact words of the lecture so you can check. Read the passages carefully: I only ring once.

Scoring

Correct: 100 points + up to 100 for speed. From the third correct answer in a row: +50 each. Wrong: 0 points, and in Solo mode you lose a life.

Solo power-ups: ½ removes 2 wrong options · ❄ freezes the clock for 15 seconds · ⤼ skips a question without losing a life.

Shortcuts: A B C D (or 1–4) to answer, Enter for the next question.
