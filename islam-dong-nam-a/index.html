Monsoon Voyage

40 questions · 8 trading ports · 5 question types: reading comprehension, text structure, paragraph summary, multiple choice, fill in the blank.

I am the Monsoon. The lecture says I "governed Indian Ocean navigation, compelling foreign merchants to reside in regional port cities for months at a time". In other words, merchants had to wait for me, and while they waited, Islam took root.

Today I will carry you through eight trading ports, five questions at each. After every answer I read you the exact words of the lecture so you can check. Read the passages carefully: I blow fast.

Scoring

Correct: 100 points + up to 100 for speed. From the third correct answer in a row: +50 each. Wrong: 0 points, and in Solo mode you lose a life.

Solo power-ups: ½ removes 2 wrong options · ❄ freezes the clock for 15 seconds · ⤼ skips a question without losing a life.
